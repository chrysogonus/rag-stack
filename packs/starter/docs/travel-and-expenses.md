# Travel and expenses

## Booking travel

Book trains and flights through the travel desk at least 14 days ahead. Take the train for any trip shorter than four hours.

## Hotels

The company pays up to 150 EUR a night for a hotel. In London, Paris and Zurich the limit is 220 EUR a night.

## Meals

Meals on a business trip are covered up to 30 EUR a day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Expense reports

Submit an expense report with a photo of every receipt within 30 days of the trip. Your manager approves it, and the money reaches your account with the next salary payment.
