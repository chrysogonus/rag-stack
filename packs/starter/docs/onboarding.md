# Onboarding

Welcome to the team. This page covers your first week.

## Your first day

You get your laptop and accounts from the IT desk at 9:00. Your manager introduces you to your buddy, a colleague who answers your questions during your first month.

## Accounts

You get an email account, a chat account and a seat in the password manager on your first day. Turn on two-factor authentication for each of them before the day ends (see the security guide).

## Your first week

By Friday of your first week you have met your team, set up your development environment and shipped one small change, reviewed by your buddy.
