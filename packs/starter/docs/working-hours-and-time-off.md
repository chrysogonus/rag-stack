# Working hours and time off

## Core hours

Everyone is available between 10:00 and 15:00 on working days. Outside core hours you plan your own time.

## Remote work

You can work from home up to three days a week. Working from abroad needs your manager's approval and is limited to 20 working days a year.

## Vacation

You have 28 vacation days a year. Request vacation in the HR tool at least two weeks ahead. Up to five unused days carry over until March 31 of the next year.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, tell your manager before 9:00. From the fourth day of sickness on, you need a doctor's note.
